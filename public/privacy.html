1. Introduction

Echo Show Remote ("we", "our", "the app") is a companion remote-control application for Amazon Echo Show and other Alexa-enabled devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information.

By using Echo Show Remote and its associated Alexa Skill, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app or the skill.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers unless you enable premium features:

  • App theme, background choice, and haptic-feedback preference
  • Selected Echo Show generation
  • Local network device IP and port (only if you use the Local Server backend)
  • Alexa command history (last 20 commands, for quick-reply convenience)
  • Custom macros and scenes you create
  • Parental PIN, kid-mode state, accessibility toggle
  • Sleep/wake schedule times

2.2 Data stored on our servers (only when you link Alexa)

If you enable the Echo Show Remote Alexa Skill and complete account linking, we store the following on our servers to route commands from your phone to your Echo devices:

  • An anonymous user identifier issued when you link your Alexa account
  • The OAuth access token and refresh token that Alexa issues to the Skill so it can send events on your behalf
  • Timestamp of when you linked your account

We do not store your Amazon email address, password, or any personally identifying information from your Amazon account.

2.3 Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • No Amazon account credentials (all authentication is handled directly by Amazon)
  • No voice recordings — we never receive or store any audio
  • No smart home device inventory — commands are relayed only, never inspected
  • No location data, contacts, or photos
  • No advertising identifiers

3. Alexa account linking

When you link your Alexa account to Echo Show Remote:

  • You are redirected to Amazon's official OAuth 2.0 authorization page
  • Amazon (not us) prompts you to sign in and grant permission
  • Amazon issues our Alexa Skill an access token and refresh token scoped only to sending Skill Events to your account's Echo devices
  • We store these tokens securely and use them only to forward commands you initiate in the app

You can revoke this access at any time by disabling the Echo Show Remote Skill in your Alexa app, or by tapping Unlink Alexa Account in the app's Settings.

4. How we use your data

We use the limited data we collect only to:

  • Route commands from your phone to your Echo devices via Amazon's Skill Messaging API
  • Persist your app settings across app launches on your device
  • Validate promotional codes you enter
  • Provide basic error diagnostics (server error logs are retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted)

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not train machine learning models on your data.

5. Where your data is stored

Server-side data (Alexa tokens and anonymous user IDs) is stored in encrypted Vercel KV (Redis) databases hosted in the United States and protected by industry-standard access controls. All connections between your app, our servers, and Amazon use HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher.

6. Data sharing

We share data only with:

  • Amazon — command payloads sent to the Alexa Skill Messaging API to control your Echo devices
  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which processes server-side requests

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any other third party. We do not disclose your data to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

We will disclose data only when required by law (subpoena, court order) and will notify you first unless legally prohibited from doing so.

7. Your rights & controls

You can at any time:

  • Access the data associated with your account by emailing us
  • Delete your account and all server-side data by disabling the Alexa Skill or emailing us
  • Clear all on-device data by uninstalling the app
  • Export your macros and settings via Settings → Data

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the additional rights to correction, portability, and objection to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's privacy

Echo Show Remote is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional Kid Mode feature restricts content displayed within the app but does not itself collect any child-specific data. If we learn that we've inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

9. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. If we make material changes, we will notify you in-app and via a prominent banner on this page 30 days before the changes take effect.
